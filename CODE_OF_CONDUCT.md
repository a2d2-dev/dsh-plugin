# Code of Conduct

We want contributors and users to work together respectfully across languages, backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be considerate, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and code with evidence.
- Respect people's privacy and boundaries. Do not publish personal information, credentials or private conversations without permission.
- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, personal attacks and sustained disruption are not acceptable.
- Assume good intent when clarifying a disagreement, and follow reasonable moderation requests.

This policy applies to the repository's Issues, pull requests and other project-managed collaboration spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation or block accounts as appropriate to the conduct and its impact. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so will not expose private information or worsen harm.

For a conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through an available GitHub profile contact route. If none is available, request a private contact channel in a minimal Issue without identifying affected people or publishing the report. Immediate platform abuse can also be reported through GitHub's reporting tools. Maintainers should avoid deciding reports in which they have a conflict of interest.
